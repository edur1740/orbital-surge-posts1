The game

Four ways to win a match

Every match is 2 minutes 30 seconds of choices. Feed the core or haul rods? Surge now or wait? Defend, or race home to climb?

Feed the core

Launch glowing plasma cells into the shared Reactor Core at center field.

3 AUTO · 1 TELEOP

Build the array

Haul fuel rods from your airlock across the field and stack them on three levels. Three in a row makes a LINK.

ROD 2–7 · LINK +5

Time the surge

Every 15 cells charges a SURGE. Your human player hits the button and rods score double for 12 seconds.

RODS ×2

Climb the tower

In the last 25 seconds, race home and haul yourself up the tether tower.
